Saving a clip while driving

Each save keeps the 3 minutes before and the minute after. On the comma screen, hold a finger on the road view for a second, or swipe left from the right edge.

Steering wheel button (hold for a second to save)
Takes effect the next time you start the car.
Saving a video to your iPhone
  1. Tap Save on a clip and wait while it's prepared (a few seconds).
  2. Tap Download when Safari asks.
  3. Open the Files app → Downloads, tap the video, then Share → Save Video to put it in Photos.
Upload to your own server

Saved clips are copied to your server over WebDAV (Nextcloud, Synology, TrueNAS and most home-cloud software support it). These settings are stored only on this device.

The away address starts with http://, so your password would cross the internet unencrypted. Use https:// if your server supports it.
